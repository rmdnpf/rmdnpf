```
Bold 1980s Japanese anime movie poster ink illustration inspired by the 1988 anime Akira, thick black ink linework and solid black shapes,

no gradients, no painting, no 3D render, no text, no watermark, no ground shadow,
plain flat pure white background (#FFFFFF), the whole subject fully visible with generous empty space on all sides.
```
